# Cross-asset crisis trend v1

One fixed candidate was registered before measurement: 80% equal weight across the five saved strategies other than `sec-growth-survivorship-aware-v1`, plus 20% causal long-only trend across bonds, credit, metals, commodities and the dollar.

## Result

- All six saved strategies: 45.50% CAGR, 2.17 Sharpe, -20.45% maximum drawdown, -8.34% worst rolling year.
- Five-sleeve base: 47.50% CAGR, 2.40 Sharpe, -18.11% maximum drawdown, -7.33% worst rolling year.
- Five-sleeve base on the candidate's identical 180-week window: 45.47% CAGR, 2.34 Sharpe, -18.11% maximum drawdown, -7.33% worst rolling year.
- Fixed 80/20 candidate at 50 bps trend costs: 36.68% CAGR, 2.39 Sharpe, -14.87% maximum drawdown, -5.23% worst rolling year.

The excluded growth/Micron sleeve contributed 14.2% of equal-weight arithmetic return and 25.7% of variance risk on the common window. That confirms the direction of the removal decision, but not the different figures in the external summary.

Decision: **reject_as_portfolio_improvement**. This is retrospective research on strategies already selected from the same 2023-2026 window. It cannot alter the September 4 registry, start a forward clock, authorize promotion, or support live trading. The project-wide attempt count is not reproducible, so the raw block-bootstrap result is not presented as multiplicity-adjusted evidence.
