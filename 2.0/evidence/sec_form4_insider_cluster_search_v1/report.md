# SEC Form 4 insider-cluster search v1

Tested 5,760 costed overlays from 2,560 point-in-time open-market purchase filings. The best discovery path produced 97.36% recent CAGR, 2.78 Sharpe, -9.13% drawdown, and 37.38% full CAGR. At 200-bps costs it retained 91.32%.

Only original Form 4 code-P acquisitions with positive shares and prices were included. Filing dates are conservatively eligible only on a later weekly decision. This search cannot authorize promotion before delay, issuer-exclusion, and neighboring-parameter audits.
