# SEC growth survivorship-aware retest v1

The unchanged growth rule was tested across **58** point-in-time quarterly decisions. At 50-bps costs, the base scenario produced **32.40% CAGR**, **0.966 Sharpe**, and **-37.24% maximum drawdown**. The mandatory adverse missing-company scenario produced **17.30% CAGR**, **0.612 Sharpe**, and **-47.32% drawdown**. SPY returned **22.32% CAGR** on the same weekly span.

From the old pilot's holdout start, the survivorship-aware base CAGR was **38.10%** versus the old 20-survivor pilot's **36.14%**. The old pilot is not used as a valid benchmark claim. No strategy is automatically promoted; the paired result and validation checks determine the next decision.
