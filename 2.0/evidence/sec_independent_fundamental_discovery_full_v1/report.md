# Independent SEC fundamental discovery v1

Five predeclared non-growth families were tested on 58 point-in-time decisions with a fixed top-10 portfolio. The strongest trailing-year result was **balance_sheet_quality** at **67.11% CAGR**, **1.716 Sharpe**, and **-16.89% drawdown**. Its weekly correlation with the frozen SEC growth sleeve was **0.591**, and its adverse full-period CAGR was **21.55%**.

This is a discovery result, not a promotion. Valuation was excluded because the available adjusted-price/share-count combination is not corporate-action consistent.
